A Quick Update!

Whether you’re new here or you’ve been with me since the Stone Age, welcome!

I wanted to take a moment to thank you for subscribing to Book Zone Reviews (BZR).

BZR is where I share reviews of books for readers ages 0–17, along with the occasional author interview, podcast highlight, and reading recommendations.

I’ve also clarified the roles of my two book-related sites. Going forward, blitzes, cover reveals, and introductions to new books will appear on The Book Zone, while Book Zone Reviews will focus on reviews and recommendations designed to help readers discover books worth reading.

If you’re looking for your child’s next great read—or simply enjoy discovering new books—I hope you’ll stick around. I believe the best days of BZR are still ahead.

Thank you for allowing me into your inbox and for being part of BZR’s journey.

Blessings,

Dee S.
